About Khafaia · خَفايا

Look closer. Find the hidden value.

KHAFAIA is an Omani circular-economy platform focused on palm waste across species and stages of cultivation, maintenance, harvesting and processing.

We bring together waste generators, manufacturers, researchers and entrepreneurs to explore material recovery and develop practical commercial opportunities—grounded in evidence, local context and real-world feasibility.

Material-first

Start with the biomass and its actual properties.

Evidence-aware

Ideas are research directions until validated.

Locally rooted

Designed around Oman's resources and communities.

Circular by nature

Help useful biomass find a productive next life.

Every palm holds a possibility.

Bring a material, a question or an idea. Begin exploring where it could lead.

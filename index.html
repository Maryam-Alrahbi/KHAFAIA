About KHAFAIA · خَفايا

Look closer.
Find the hidden value.

KHAFAIA is an Omani circular-economy platform focused on palm waste across species and stages of cultivation, maintenance, harvesting and processing.

We bring together waste generators, manufacturers, researchers and entrepreneurs to explore material recovery and develop practical commercial opportunities—grounded in evidence, local context and real-world feasibility.

Material-firstStart with the biomass and its actual properties.
Evidence-awareIdeas are research directions until validated.
Locally rootedDesigned around Oman's resources and communities.
Circular by natureHelp useful biomass find a productive next life.
